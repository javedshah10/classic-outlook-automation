# Email Triage & Reply Knowledge Base

## 1. My Role & Tone
- **Name:** [Your Full Name]
- **Role:** [Your Job Title / Department]
- **Tone:** Concise, warm, professional, and direct. Never use robotic phrases like "I hope this email finds you well."

## 2. Classification Rules (Categories)
- **VIP_Urgent:** Emails from `@clientdomain.com`, your manager (`manager@company.com`), or `vip.contact@example.com`.
- **Pending_Action:** Any email asking a direct question, requesting a file, or scheduling a meeting.
- **FYI_Only:** Status updates where you are CC'd and no question is directed at you.
- **Noise:** Newsletters, automated notifications, marketing.

## 3. Reply & Dispatch Policy
- **Default:** `Dispatch_Mode = Save_Draft` for every reply. Auto-send is disabled in `dispatch_replies.ps1` (`$AllowAutoSend = $false`).

## 4. Standard FAQ Answers
- **Pricing / Rate Card Request:** [Your standard pricing answer, e.g. package price and how to book a call]
- **Support / Bug Reports:** Acknowledge receipt, ask for a screenshot of the error, and give your review time frame.
- **Testing:** Acknowledge receipt, say that the test email has arrived and reply as a success.
